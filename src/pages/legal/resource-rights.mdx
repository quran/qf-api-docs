# Content rights by resource

The [Developer Terms](/legal/developer-terms) govern use of QF Content in an Application. Certain resources have their own underlying rights holders and may carry additional restrictions. This table summarizes how the Terms apply to resource types; it does not change the permissions in the Terms or the conditions attached to a particular resource.

| Resource | Use and storage under the Developer Terms | Additional considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quran text and Mushaf layout data | Display in the Application without modifying Quran text. Content available through [Content Sync](/docs/tutorials/content-sync/getting-started#content-available-for-offline-sync) may be stored under Section 3.1's ongoing sync conditions. | A prepackaged content database is not authorized merely by Content Sync; Section 2.2 governs redistribution. |
| Translations, tafsir, and word-by-word content | Display as part of the Application. Apply the Content Sync exception only to content available through it; otherwise the one-week storage limit applies unless QF permits longer storage. | An author, translator, or publisher may hold underlying rights and impose additional conditions. |
| Recitation metadata and audio-file rows | Use the API data in the Application; apply Content Sync conditions to eligible rows. | A row or URL identifies a recording but is not itself a statement of the rights in the recording. |
| Underlying audio recordings | Their use is subject to the Developer Terms and any conditions applicable to the recording. | A reciter, producer, or publisher may hold underlying rights. Distinguish the recording from its API metadata. |
| Font files and Mushaf images obtained through QF APIs or documented CDN URLs | Section 3.1 expressly permits caching and bundling for use in an Application with an active Developer Console account and QF credit. | The files may be distributed only as an integrated part of the Application, not as a separate asset offering. |
| Articles and reflections | Use within the Application under the Terms. Content Sync storage applies only where the resource is available through Content Sync. | Respect any applicable author rights and the Developer Privacy Policy Requirements for User Data. |

For a proposed use beyond the Developer Terms, contact [developers@quran.com](mailto:developers@quran.com) with the resource and intended use.
