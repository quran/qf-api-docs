# Content rights by resource

This matrix explains how the [Developer Terms](/legal/developer-terms) apply to common Content API resources. It is **not** a list of blanket copyright clearances or a substitute for a resource's own license. Check the source, edition, attribution, license notice, and any additional restrictions for the **particular resource ID** you use. Availability through an API or [Content Sync](/docs/tutorials/content-sync/getting-started#content-available-for-offline-sync) establishes a way to access or update data, not ownership or permission to redistribute it. Where a notice is absent or ambiguous, seek confirmation from [developers@quran.com](mailto:developers@quran.com) before relying on additional rights. The IDs below are examples used in the Content Sync guide, **not** a statement that a particular underlying rightsholder has cleared additional uses.

## Per-resource rights status and lookup

The published API documentation does not currently certify an individual source-holder license for the example IDs below. Their **documented permission is the in-app use under Section 2.2**, subject to the source's restrictions and Section 3.1. “Not verified here” means no additional offline-media, bundling, redistribution, or AI-training right is granted by this matrix; it does not claim that no separate license exists. For each resource, record the ID, named source/edition, any source notice, and written permission **before** relying on an additional right. If the linked endpoint provides only descriptive information, do not treat that description as a license.

Before publishing broader per-ID grants, QF must verify and maintain each rightsholder, license or written authorization, attribution text, permitted uses, and effective/revocation dates. If those facts are unavailable, the safe published status is **no additional rights verified**; contact QF and the applicable source holder for the proposed use.

## Rules by resource type

Where the table says “seven-day sync,” the ongoing schedule applies when connectivity to QF permits. A previously synced Content Sync copy may remain available during a longer connectivity interruption, but the Application must attempt sync promptly on reconnection and apply all changes; see Section 3.1 of the [Developer Terms](/legal/developer-terms). This exception does not extend the one-week limit for content outside Content Sync or grant rights to audio files referenced by sync rows.

| Resource / examples | Ordinary in-app use under the Terms | Copying, offline use, and distribution | Rights and attribution to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `quran_core:1` and Quran text through regular endpoints | Display canonical text without modifying it, with context preserved. | Content Sync rows may be cached with the ongoing seven-day sync requirement; integrated prepackaged databases require express written permission, while independently reusable copies require a signed commercial license under Section 2.2. Other text variants not in Content Sync have the one-week limit unless separately permitted. | Verify the specific text edition and applicable source/attribution notice. Do not assume all variants have identical rights. |

Prepackaged layout databases require written permission. | Check the edition and any underlying layout or publisher restrictions. |
| Font files and Mushaf images from QF APIs or documented CDN URLs | Render in the Application. | Section 3.1 permits caching and integrated app bundles with an active Developer Console account and QF credit, subject to source-specific terms. No standalone asset download or package. | Check provider, license name/URL, and attribution when provided for the particular asset. |
| `translations`, `word_by_word_translations`, `word_by_word_transliterations`, `tafsirs` | Display the selected edition in context and credit its named source. | Eligible Content Sync rows may be cached with seven-day sync; no prepackaged database or reusable feed without written permission. Sync does **not** grant a license to package or redistribute the recordings linked by those rows. | Confirm reciter, recording producer/publisher, edition, attribution, and any restrictions for the particular recording. | **Underlying recitation recordings** (the media reached via an audio URL) | In-app streaming/playback is permitted subject to that recording's source rights and credit. | Do not infer offline-download, permanent media-cache, preloaded audio bundle, remix, separate audio feed, or recording redistribution rights from an audio URL. Obtain rights covering the particular use and comply with the applicable storage limit. | Recording and performance rights may be held by parties other than QF. | `articles` and reflections or other user-generated material | Display within permitted Application features with source context. | Sync-eligible article rows can follow seven-day sync; other material has the ordinary one-week limit unless expressly permitted. No bulk export or prepackaged corpus. | Confirm author/publisher rights for articles, and user consent and privacy obligations for reflections and User Data. |

**How to use the matrix:** Identify the precise resource or asset and its source terms; distinguish API metadata from files reached through a URL; determine whether it is actually eligible for Content Sync; then apply the stricter of these Terms and the applicable source-specific conditions. A grant from QF cannot override a third party's rights. If your use requires a content package, offline audio library, AI training dataset, or other right not expressly granted, obtain a written agreement covering that resource and the relevant rights holders before distributing or retaining it. Independently reusable content or raw API data also requires the signed commercial license in Section 2.2.
