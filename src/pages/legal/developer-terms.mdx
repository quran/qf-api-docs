#### Storage and offline copies

If a content resource is available through Content Sync, **Content Sync is the only permitted path for obtaining and maintaining an offline copy** of that resource; do not build an offline copy from regular API responses. When using Content Sync, you must [perform a next sync](/docs/tutorials/content-sync/getting-started#next-sync) at least every **7 days when connectivity to Quran Foundation permits** and apply all available changes. This ongoing obligation applies to copies kept on a backend or an offline device. If connectivity to Quran Foundation is unavailable for longer than seven days, the Application may continue using its previously synced Content Sync copy; perform a next sync promptly when connectivity returns and apply the available changes.

:::important Offline content: scholarly review and corrections

**Providing scholarly-verified Quranic content is one of Quran Foundation's core goals.** Our scholarly team reviews our content, and we publish updates and corrections as that work continues. For resources available through Content Sync, syncing delivers those updates and corrections to your Application's offline copy, helping users read current content rather than an outdated cached version. Follow the synchronization requirements above to keep that copy up to date.

:::

**Offline recitation downloads (no 1-week limit on devices).** An Application may let end users download recitation audio for offline listening and keep it on their device for as long as they want, with no 1-week limit. - (e) the Application deletes the files when the end user removes them, when Quran Foundation asks for their removal, or when the Developer's access under these Terms ends.

Files past an applicable limit must be deleted; stopping playback is not sufficient.
