# Mushaf Fonts and Images

Content Sync provides Quran text and Mushaf layout data and may include font asset metadata such as CDN URLs. Font files themselves and page images are delivered separately and are not included in Content Sync.

Use the [Font Rendering guide](/docs/tutorials/fonts/font-rendering/) to load supported fonts from Quran Foundation’s official CDN.

Fonts and images remain subject to their providers’ conditions. Quran Foundation does not issue separate licences on behalf of those providers. Contact Quran Foundation developer support only for broken documented URLs or incorrect rendering.

## Standard use

When using a documented Quran Foundation CDN font:

- Use it only inside your application to render Quran content.
- Do not sell, modify, rehost, or offer the font as a separate download.
- Follow the provider-specific conditions below.
