## Obtaining OAuth 2.0 client credentials

> A prerequisite to creating client credentials is for your app to have the value of the redirect URL where the user will land after successfully authenticating/logging out implemented.

Once you have the redirect URL, [create your OAuth2 app in Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects/new) and register it there.

For a confidential backend/server app, keep the login screen and PKCE flow in your mobile app, but perform the authorization-code exchange and refresh-token exchange on your backend. For a public browser/mobile app, use a fully public in-app token exchange flow without a client secret.
