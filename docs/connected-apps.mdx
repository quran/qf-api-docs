# Connected Apps

> **Policy version:** 1.1 · **Last updated:** 2026-08-10 · **Effective date:** 2026-08-10

**Partner and developer guide**

Connected Apps is the Quran Foundation program for developers and app owners who
want to build with Quran Foundation APIs, connect Quran.com accounts, and be
discoverable on [Quran.com/apps](https://quran.com/apps). This page is the
**source of truth for the Connected Apps program**: statuses, review,
governance, and what a listing or label does and does not mean. It includes the
onboarding steps developers need for a head start; the broader developer
experience, including API references, SDK guides, and Developer Console
documentation, is covered in its own areas of the documentation.

## Start here

**Three actions cover most visits to this page:**

- **Build with Quran Foundation APIs**: sign in to the
  [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects) with your Quran
  Foundation account, create your app, and get credentials.
- **List your app on Quran.com/apps**: check the
  [four eligibility gates](#check-whether-your-app-is-eligible), then
  [prepare and submit a listing package](#prepare-your-listing).
- **Connect Quran.com accounts**: read the
  [OAuth requirements](#connect-qurancom-user-accounts) and request scopes in
  the Developer Console.

:::info Does your app use AI?
AI-generated explanations, labels, summaries, verse connections, or
pre-generated religious content carry additional hard requirements and review.
Read [AI features and generated religious content](#ai-features-and-generated-religious-content)
before you build.
:::

### Who should use this guide

- **App builders**: You discovered Connected Apps while developing a new product
  and are building with the ecosystem in mind from the outset. Your goal is to
  align with its core principles and design your app around meaningful,
  transformational user journeys from day one.
- **App owners**: You already have an existing app and have since discovered the
  Connected Apps ecosystem. You may be looking to join for greater reach and
  visibility, to evolve your product toward more transformational user journeys,
  or both. The onboarding process helps explore how your existing product can
  align with the ecosystem's direction and requirements.
- **Content partners**: You may already have an app, own or manage content that
  could be shared with the wider ecosystem, or both. Content sharing is handled
  through the separate Content Partnership Program, which involves additional
  governance around areas such as content verification, rights, licensing, and
  distribution. If you also operate an app, however, you are equally welcome to
  explore joining the Connected Apps ecosystem. At the app level, the onboarding
  route is broadly the same, although the relationship with content partners is
  often more closely coordinated because app integration and content-sharing
  arrangements may need to be considered together.

## Why build with Quran Foundation

- **Trusted infrastructure**: build faster on comprehensive APIs and scholarly
  verified Quran data, without maintaining fragile source pipelines on your own.
- **A connected ecosystem**: the Connected Apps program is more than an API
  program; it helps users move between Quran.com and participating apps without
  starting over.
- **Account continuity**: with OAuth2 and approved User APIs, bookmarks,
  progress, goals, notes, and preferences can follow the user where continuity
  is appropriate.
- **Discoverability**: qualified apps can be listed on Quran.com/apps within a
  trusted directory, without discovery ever being paid placement.
- **Direct support**: guidance from Quran Foundation and a mission-aligned
  network of builders through access, OAuth, listing, and review decisions.

### A curated ecosystem built on ta'awun

The Connected Apps program is a highly curated ecosystem. Its basis is mutual
ta'awun (cooperation) and trust between builders, Quran Foundation, and its
team. Every app that is listed goes through a manual review by the Quran
Foundation developer relations team; no app enters the directory through an
automated pipeline. We strive to build a personal bond that goes beyond
technical integration: guidance through onboarding, scholarly feedback on your
features, technical support, and feedback logging as your product grows. When
you join the program, you are not integrating with an API surface; you are
joining a team that wants your app to succeed in serving the Ummah.

## The Developer Console

The [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects) is where you manage
your relationship with Quran Foundation as a developer. It replaces the previous
request-access flow for acquiring API credentials. Sign in with a Quran
Foundation account, then:

- **Create an app** and get API credentials for the **prelive (testing)** and
  **production** environments.
- **Claim an existing client** created before the console launched and manage it
  in the console from then on.
- **Manage credentials**: rotate your client secret whenever needed.
- **Manage redirect URLs**: add and remove redirect URIs at will.
- **Request API scopes** for prelive and production, and **track your requested
  scopes** and their status.
- **Check your activity log** and switch easily between apps if you work on more
  than one.

The console also carries the developer documentation you need along the way:
links to the API docs and official SDKs, the developer privacy policy and terms
of service, technical support guidance, and the developer benefits and
responsibilities summarized on the apps dashboard view. More capabilities are
planned. Detailed console guides live in the developer documentation; this page
covers the console only as it relates to the Connected Apps program.

:::note
Credentials, redirect URIs, and scope requests are self-service in the console.
**Listing approval, reviewed labels, status reviews, and featured placement
remain reviewed decisions**; the sections below explain how those reviews
work.
:::

## Follow the Connected Apps process

One journey takes an app from first credentials to a live listing. Review is a
sequence of decisions, not a single approval; knowing the stages helps you move
quickly.

**Sign in and create your app → Build and test in prelive → Prepare your
listing → Submit for review → Review → Go live → Keep it current**

1. **Sign in and create your app.** Open the
   [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects), create your app, and
   get prelive credentials. Use the
   [Developer Journey](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/developer-journey/)
   guide to choose Content APIs, Search APIs, User APIs, OAuth2, or SDKs for
   your app shape.
2. **Build and test in prelive.** Plan OAuth clients, redirect URIs, and
   user-data scopes early; request production scopes in the console when ready.
3. **Prepare your listing.** Assemble the
   [listing package](#prepare-your-listing) so reviewers can understand the
   product, verify ownership and support routes, and check content and data use.
4. **Submit for review.** Send the complete package (see
   [How to submit](#how-to-submit)). Incomplete or unclear packages are returned
   and slow the process.
5. **Review.** A completeness check first, then contextual review against the
   [eligibility gates](#check-whether-your-app-is-eligible) and
   [review criteria](#what-review-looks-at). Every review is carried out
   manually by the Quran Foundation developer relations team. The outcome is
   listing approval, a request for changes, or, where relevant, a label or
   status decision with its own requirements.
6. **Go live.** Approved apps appear in the directory. Labels and featuring are
   separate, additional decisions.
7. **Keep it current.** Some changes
   [require review before shipping](#tell-us-before-shipping-material-changes).

**Timelines.** Quran Foundation aims to acknowledge submissions within 1 to 2
business days and return an initial review within 5 business days of receiving a
complete package. Complex content, OAuth, AI, data, or commercial cases may take
longer. For status, contact <developers@quran.com>.

### What review looks at

Review is contextual. The same product can be suitable for a directory listing
but not for OAuth scopes, homepage placement, or a reviewed label.

- **Mission and usefulness**: does the product solve a real user need and
  support Quran reading, listening, understanding, memorization, reflection, or
  continuity?
- **Content and attribution**: are Quranic text, translations, tafsir,
  recitations, and derived content sourced, represented, kept current, and
  attributed accurately?
- **Data and consent**: are requested scopes proportionate, explained clearly,
  secured appropriately, and limited to the product function?
- **Product quality**: does the product work reliably, avoid avoidable
  confusion, and provide a support route for users who need help?
- **Commercial model**: are charges, ads, donations, sponsorships, or affiliate
  models transparent, proportionate, and compatible with content obligations?
- **Ongoing accountability**: can the app owner keep listing details current,
  respond to issues, and cooperate with reasonable review or corrective action?

## Check whether your app is eligible

Some checks are contextual and weighed in review. These four are not. They are
**pass/fail**, and they are the baseline protections for users, Quran
Foundation, and the integrity of Quranic content. No app may be indexed or
listed, receive a reviewed label, reach Transformational status, or receive
featured placement until all four gates are met; even the lowest-visibility
status requires full content-sourcing validation and governance compliance. If
an app later falls out of compliance, the same gates govern restriction or
removal.

### Gate 1: Content integrity and updates

- **You must**: access Quran Foundation-provided Quranic content through
  approved Quran Foundation endpoints or a written license; hold verifiable
  rights, accurate attribution, and any category-specific approval for external
  Quranic, scholarly, or supporting content; and keep all content current where
  corrections, removals, restrictions, or source updates apply.
- **Evidence**: API usage, release notes, update cadence, in-app attribution,
  and license or rights records.
- **If unmet**: unlisted or delisted until corrected.

### Gate 2: Security and privacy baseline

- **You must**: publish a privacy notice, maintain a reasonable security
  posture, avoid harmful or deceptive data practices, and provide a security or
  incident contact.
- **Evidence**: privacy link, security contact, data minimization, and an
  incident-response route.
- **If unmet**: unlisted or delisted.

### Gate 3: API and platform compliance

- **You must**: respect API terms, usage rules, branding rules, and licensing or
  attribution terms.
- **Evidence**: accepted partner terms, compliance acknowledgement, and
  monitored usage.
- **If unmet**: unlisted or delisted.

### Gate 4: Maintenance and responsiveness

- **You must**: name a technical contact who can respond to breaking changes
  within agreed timelines.
- **Evidence**: support route, issue responsiveness, and maintenance commitment.
- **If unmet**: unlisted or delisted.

:::note
Eligibility is judged on its own terms. A strong marketing presence, large user
base, or popularity does not offset a failed gate.
:::

## Prepare your listing

The public [Quran.com/apps](https://quran.com/apps) page is designed for user
discovery, not internal review history. A directory card should help users
answer five questions quickly: what the app helps them do, who owns and supports
it, which platforms it runs on, whether it connects to their Quran.com account
or requests data access, and where its privacy, support, and product information
lives.

### The listing package

**Required for review**

- Product name, short descriptor or tagline, short description, and primary
  category.
- Square logo or icon with safe contrast and confirmed source rights.
- Working web, iOS, and/or Android destination links.
- Product owner, technical contact, support contact, and public support route.
- Privacy notice and terms where applicable.
- Content-source declaration for Quran Foundation APIs, licensed content,
  partner content, or other sources.
- Clear explanation of Quran Foundation authentication, scopes, or user-data
  use.

**Recommended for a stronger listing**

- One plain-language differentiator.
- Platform and language availability.
- High-quality screenshots or preview media.
- Accessibility and age-appropriateness notes.
- Accurate content attribution where surfaced in product.
- Current release, maintenance, and support status.

### Card specs

The Quran.com/apps card renders an icon, name, short descriptor, description,
categories, and CTA links. The product UI clamps names and taglines to one line
and descriptions to three lines, so keep submissions short enough to stay
readable on mobile and desktop.

| Field | Required | Format or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App name | Yes | 40 characters maximum; exactly as it should appear publicly. |
| Short descriptor / tagline | Yes | 60 characters maximum; descriptive, not promotional; displays on one line. |
| Short description | Yes | 1 to 2 sentences, 160 characters maximum; what it does and who it helps; no hype, unverifiable claims, or repeated tagline text. |
| Icon / logo | Yes | Square, minimum 512 x 512 px, PNG, SVG, or WebP; renders at about 52 x 52 px desktop and 48 x 48 px mobile; safe contrast, clean edges, confirmed rights. |
| Primary category | Yes | One approved category; drives filtering and discovery. |
| Secondary tags | Optional | Approved taxonomy terms. |
| CTA links | Yes | Valid, working, official web, iOS, and/or Android destinations. |
| Support contact and public support route | Yes | Publicly accessible support email or URL. |
| Privacy policy link | Yes | Public URL. |
| Attribution / content-source declaration | Yes | Required where Quranic, scholarly, or supporting content is used; see [Content and attribution](#content-and-attribution-requirements). |
| Technical contact / update owner | Yes | Named operational contact, supplied privately where not suitable for public display. |
| Badge / label | If displayed | Verified Listing App, Vision Aligned App, Transformational App, or Featured; set by review, not self-assigned. |

**A card that works, and one that does not:**

> **Good**: *Quran Memorization Companion*. "Review and practice memorized
> passages." "Build personalized revision plans and track your Quran
> memorization progress."

> **Poor**: *The World's #1 Revolutionary Ultimate Quran Super App*. "The best
> app for every Muslim." "Transform your life with our unmatched and
> groundbreaking platform."

### How to submit

Until listing submission moves into the Developer Console, submissions run
through the listing submission document:

1. Open the [listing submission document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Dh_dQH2hjDL9CC0gKUhMMqdR6VJ1sVu-eNJPrGb13ME/edit?usp=sharing).
2. Make a copy (File, then Make a copy) into your own Drive.
3. Complete every field, including content sources, Quran Foundation APIs used,
   OAuth scopes requested, AI features, and commercial model.
4. Email your completed copy (share link or attachment) to
   <developers@quran.com>.

**Know which request you are making.** These are different tasks with different
routes:

- **API credentials, redirect URIs, secret rotation, scope requests**: the
  [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects).
- **Submit an app listing**: email the package above to <developers@quran.com>.
- **Request a status review** (Verified Listing App, Vision Aligned App, or Transformational App): email
  <developers@quran.com> with your app name and the status you are requesting.
- **Report a material change**: email <developers@quran.com> before shipping;
  see [when review is required](#tell-us-before-shipping-material-changes).

## App statuses

Higher levels of visibility, trust, and ecosystem participation are earned
incrementally. Each status builds on the one below it and never replaces the
eligibility gates. A genuinely valuable app can remain at a lower status if it
has not implemented the integration a higher status describes; that is expected,
not a penalty.

The statuses are intentionally progressive: **Indexed (searchable) App →
Verified Listing App → Vision Aligned App → Transformational (user enabled)
App**, with **Featured placement** as a separate,
temporary editorial promotion rather than a status you hold. Content-only apps
are the normal starting point: most apps begin as Verified Listing Apps and grow
into Vision Aligned Apps as they plan meaningful continuity. Where an app has no
meaningful account-based experience to connect, Transformational status is not
expected.

:::tip The destination is Transformational
The status we want every app to reach is **Transformational**. Keep looking for
opportunities to integrate Quran Foundation content and OAuth user APIs in ways
that genuinely strengthen your product and carry users further along their
Quran journey. Deliberate, meaningful integration is the path; OAuth added
without real user value is not.
:::

### Indexed (searchable) App

- **What it means**: a low-visibility working status for apps that meet all
  governance requirements but have not yet reached the integration depth or
  transformational-funnel impact for full directory visibility. Indexed Apps do
  not appear in the browsable Connected Apps sections, but they can be found by
  name through search.
- **Requires**: all four eligibility gates, content-sourcing validation, and the
  same governance compliance as every other status. Indexed App status is not an
  exemption from requirements; it reflects current impact, not current
  compliance.
- **May say**: "indexed and searchable in the Quran.com Connected Apps
  directory."
- **Does not mean**: placement in browsable directory sections, a reviewed
  label, or any endorsement.
- **How to move up**: address the deficiencies identified in review, deepen
  meaningful integration, and request Verified Listing App review via
  <developers@quran.com>.

### Verified Listing App

- **What it means**: the baseline visible listing on the Connected Apps page,
  and where most apps start. These are typically apps using the Content APIs
  and nothing more, whether because the product has no need for user continuity
  or because continuity is not implemented yet.
- **Requires**: all requirements met, with confirmed content provenance
  (approved Quran Foundation APIs or a written license where Quran Foundation
  content is used, and verifiable rights for approved external content where it
  is used), attribution displayed throughout the app for all content used
  through Quran Foundation APIs, verified update and maintenance arrangements,
  and reliable basic UX.
- **Grants**: the Verified label and a visible listing in the directory.
- **Does not mean**: every product area, future release, or partner claim is
  approved.
- **How obtained**: request review via <developers@quran.com>.

### Vision Aligned App

- **What it means**: still a content app, but one that shows real potential for
  user continuity and is deliberately on its way to integrating meaningful
  OAuth and user-related APIs. Aligned apps demonstrate mission and vision
  alignment with Quran Foundation, share the same thinking on further
  development and integration, and serve the transformational funnel and help
  enable it further.
- **Requires**: Verified Listing App status, plus demonstrated mission alignment and clear
  user value, maintained responsible-use standards, participation in change
  management for material updates, deprecations, and content corrections, and a
  credible, deliberate path toward meaningful continuity.
- **Grants**: eligibility for curated ecosystem roundups, relevant partner
  showcases, and enhanced discovery opportunities.
- **Does not mean**: permanent status, an exclusive relationship, or Quran
  Foundation ownership.
- **How obtained**: request review via <developers@quran.com>.

This is the status we want every Verified Listing App to move toward: joining the
ecosystem through Quran Foundation SSO, one account that lets users move across
participating apps with a continued journey, powered by the user-related APIs.
The direction must be deliberate: integration that genuinely strengthens the
product, not OAuth and user APIs added without value.

### Transformational (user enabled) App

- **What it means**: the highest status. Transformational apps show deep vision
  and mission alignment with Quran Foundation, have implemented OAuth, and
  deliver immense value to the ecosystem and the transformational model. These
  are the apps Quran Foundation works with most closely: deeper collaboration,
  approved data-sharing initiatives, joint work on the transformation process,
  and support wherever Quran Foundation can help push the product further.
- **Requires**: Vision Aligned App status, plus OAuth2 integration and approved use of
  user-related APIs where continuity is relevant to the product's user journey,
  with Quran Foundation OAuth preferably the primary sign-in. Relying on Quran
  Foundation OAuth as the sole source of backend user authentication is what
  makes an app eligible for Featured placement.
- **Grants**: eligibility for enhanced placement and Featured consideration,
  subject to editorial discretion and current ecosystem priorities, and
  candidacy for deeper collaboration and approved data-sharing initiatives.
  Collaboration follows the status; it never purchases it (see
  [Visibility is not for sale](#visibility-is-not-for-sale)).
- **Does not mean**: permission to request unnecessary scopes, bypass consent,
  or reuse data outside the approved purpose.
- **How obtained**: request review via <developers@quran.com> after OAuth
  readiness (consent copy, redirect URIs, proportionate scopes, privacy
  information, data security, incident contact, and support route).

### Featured placement (temporary editorial promotion)

Featured placement is **selected, not applied for**. It is a time-boxed
editorial promotion on a specific Quran Foundation surface or campaign,
re-evaluated each cycle. It is reserved for Transformational Apps that are
aligned in vision with Quran Foundation, work closely on transformation, use
Quran Foundation OAuth as their sole source of backend user authentication, and
have demonstrated sustained user value, reliability, current availability, and
support readiness. It is not a permanent status, not paid advertising, not a
ranking guarantee, and not an ownership relationship. Partners may express
interest, and content-only apps remain eligible for directory discovery,
verification, alignment review, and curated editorial roundups where
appropriate.

### What a status does, and does not, mean

Every status level shares the same boundaries. A listing or label:

- is not a full marketing page for the app;
- is not a promise of homepage placement or traffic;
- does not mean Quran Foundation owns, operates, or supports the app;
- does not mean every current and future feature has been endorsed;
- applies only to the checks described for that status;
- does not replace the app owner's own privacy, terms, and support obligations.

### Visibility is not for sale

Directory placement, reviewed labels, badges, and featured placement are
**non-commercial**. They cannot be bought, and they cannot be improved through
donations, sponsorship, paid advertising, data-sharing or privileged-access
arrangements with Quran Foundation, or any other commercial relationship.
Ranking, labels, and featuring reflect eligibility, integration depth, trust,
and demonstrated user value, nothing else. A fundraising campaign, for example
on a crowdfunding platform, does not by itself disqualify an app, but
fundraising language and campaign behavior must still meet the same mission,
transparency, and responsible-use standards.

### Directory listing versus homepage promotion

| Question | Connected Apps directory | Quran.com homepage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Help users browse and evaluate relevant Quran-related apps. | Introduce a broad audience to a selected, high-confidence experience. |
| Default expectation | Eligible reviewed apps can be listed with categories and platform links. | Placement is selective, time-sensitive, and not guaranteed by listing approval. |
| Data shape | Title, tagline, description, icon, categories, relationship type, platform links. | Fewer, concise, current cards appropriate for broad Quran.com traffic. |
| Change control | Listing details should stay current and may be paused when required. | Placement can change with review, quality, editorial priorities, timing, or product status. |

## Connect Quran.com user accounts

**Do you need OAuth?** If your app has per-user, account-based features (Quran
Foundation account continuity, personal user data, or signed-in actions), yes:
use Authorization Code with PKCE, and Transformational status becomes available.
If your app only consumes content, no: use backend Client Credentials or the
official SDK, and remain eligible for the Verified Listing App or Vision Aligned
App statuses without OAuth2.

OAuth2 is what turns a collection of apps into a connected ecosystem. Where an
app meaningfully benefits from continuity of account, bookmarks, reading
progress, notes, goals, or preferences, OAuth2 is **required for
Transformational status**, and Quran Foundation SSO as the sole backend user
authentication is required for Featured placement.

| Integration | Recommended flow |
| --- | --- |
| **User APIs** (signed-in, per-user data) | Authorization Code with **PKCE** and OpenID Connect; backend token exchange for confidential clients. |
| **Content APIs** (content only, no per-user data) | Backend **Client Credentials**, or the official SDK. |

- Create your OAuth client, manage redirect URIs, and **request scopes through
  the [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects)** for prelive and
  production; track requested scopes there.
- Request the **minimum scopes** your product needs, and explain the benefit to
  the user **before** consent.
- Content-only apps that do not need cross-app continuity remain eligible for
  the Verified Listing App and Vision Aligned App statuses without OAuth2.
- Direct in-app token exchange for public-client handling is permitted **only**
  where Quran Foundation has explicitly confirmed it for your client.
- **Scope escalation requires approval.** New or expanded user-data access must
  be reviewed and approved before release; request it in the console. Where the
  purpose materially changes, users must be shown updated consent information
  and may need to reconnect.

See the [OAuth2 / OpenID Connect tutorials](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/oidc/getting-started-with-oauth2/)
and the [OAuth2 Scopes reference](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/user_related_apis_versioned/scopes/)
for implementation detail.

## Content and attribution requirements

- **Quran Foundation-provided Quranic content**, including text, translations,
  tafsir, and recitations, must be accessed through approved Quran Foundation
  endpoints or a written license.
- **External Quranic, scholarly, or supporting content** must have verifiable
  rights, accurate attribution, and any additional approval required for its
  category.
- **Display attribution** wherever Quranic content is surfaced, both in the
  product and in listing copy.
- Provide a **content-source declaration** in your listing package stating which
  Quran Foundation APIs, licensed content, partner content, or other sources the
  app uses.
- **Keep content current**: corrections, removals, or restrictions made upstream
  should be reflected promptly so users are not left with stale material.
- Credit translations, tafsir editions, and recitations to their named source or
  edition, within their licensing terms.

Use the following attribution when Quran Foundation data is surfaced:

> Quran data provided by [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation/).

Where translations, tafsir editions, or recitations are surfaced, credit each by
its named source or edition and note that it is sourced through Quran Foundation
where applicable.

## Design a trustworthy experience

Products that handle Quranic material, user progress, religious learning, or AI
assistance need extra clarity. Build trust before asking for visibility.

### What your app must tell users

Communicate these where they matter: in the app, listing copy, consent screens,
privacy notices, terms, onboarding, support pages, or release notes.

- Who owns and operates the app.
- Where users should go for product support.
- What Quran Foundation content, APIs, or account features the app uses.
- What data or permissions are requested and why they are needed.
- Whether AI, ads, donations, purchases, or sponsorships are part of the
  experience.

**Avoid misleading claims.** Do not present an independent app as Quran
Foundation owned unless it is; do not describe a directory listing as broad
endorsement; do not stretch a reviewed label beyond the checks described for it;
do not imply homepage placement, featured status, or ranking is guaranteed; and
do not hide material changes to data use, monetization, AI behavior, or content
sources.

### Build toward trust

- Present Quranic material accurately and in appropriate context.
- Design for dignity, accessibility, and informed user choices.
- Make AI-assisted features clear about their limits.
- Separate exploratory or generated output from authoritative religious
  guidance.
- Use only the scopes, content fields, and data flows your product truly needs.

### Avoid avoidable harm

- Do not use Quranic material as a superficial sales hook.
- Avoid intrusive advertising, manipulative retention, or ambiguous sponsorship.
- Do not over-collect user data or hide permission consequences.
- Do not present verified source material and unverified output as the same kind
  of authority.
- Do not use Quran Foundation branding, content, or user data outside approved
  scope.

### Gamification

Gamification is acceptable only when it supports sincere, respectful,
non-coercive progress. The goal is gentle encouragement, not spiritual pressure.

- **Helpful**: reminders, milestones, memorization support, and reflection
  prompts that preserve sincerity and dignity.
- **Needs caution**: streaks and badges must never shame users, imply spiritual
  rank, or create coercive loss-aversion around acts of worship.
- **Avoid**: FOMO-heavy countdowns, manipulative push tactics, vanity
  leaderboards for sacred acts, and deceptive scarcity or reward framing that
  cheapens religious practice.

Any mechanic that changes religious behavior, drives social comparison, or
applies emotional pressure should be flagged for review before launch or
featuring.

## AI features and generated religious content

AI features that touch Quranic material, religious learning, or user data carry
the highest trust risk in the ecosystem. The requirements below are **hard
requirements**, not guidance. They apply whether the AI runs live in the product
or is used to pre-generate content that ships as if curated, such as labels,
verse connections, summaries, or explanations.

:::caution
**AI must never replace or modify canonical source material.** Quranic Arabic,
named translations, tafsir quotations, and sourced hadith material must be
rendered verbatim from verified Quran Foundation or approved sources.
AI-generated commentary may help users navigate, compare, summarize concepts, or
explain context, but it must be clearly identified, visibly separated from
source material, grounded in real citations where it makes claims, and never
presented as a substitute for, alteration of, or binding interpretation of those
sources.
:::

| Area | Hard requirement | What reviewers look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disclosure | Clearly indicate when an answer or explanation is AI-generated, so users never mistake it for verified source material. | Visible labeling of generated output in the product. |
| Separation | Visibly distinguish retrieved source material (verse text, translation, tafsir, hadith) from any AI-generated explanation around it. | Distinct presentation; the two are never the same kind of authority. |
| Provenance | Where an AI feature makes a Quranic or scholarly claim, show the citation it rests on; every reference must resolve to a genuine, verifiable source. | Real, resolvable citations; fabricated or approximate references are a violation. |
| Authority | AI output must not be presented as binding religious authority; no fatwas, halal or haram verdicts, or personalized religious directives. | Product copy and behavior; no "verified" or "scholarship" claims without the corresponding human review. |
| Neutrality | On sectarian or madhhab-sensitive questions, AI must not assert a single position as the Islamic view; defer to qualified scholars or present recognized views without endorsing one. | Behavior on contested prompts. |

| Data and providers | Do not train models on Quran Foundation user data or restricted content without written permission; disclose third-party processing, minimize prompt data, configure providers not to retain or train on that data, name material subprocessors, and disclose material model or provider changes. | Privacy notice, subprocessor list, provider configuration. |
| Safety | AI features must not produce content that misrepresents Islam, promotes extremism, or incites sectarian hatred. | A hard line in a sacred context, not an edge case. |

## Resolve compliance issues

Enforcement is transparent, documented, and proportional, while remaining
capable of swift action for serious harm. Issues are handled along a defined
ladder:

1. **Notice**: Quran Foundation notifies the app owner of the issue and records
   the concern.
2. **Remediation window**: a timeline to fix the issue, set by severity.
3. **Visibility restriction**: if unresolved, the app may lose its badge,
   placement, featuring, category visibility, or connected access.
4. **Suspension or delisting**: serious or repeated breaches can lead to removal
   from the directory and revocation of access where appropriate.
5. **Reinstatement**: visibility or access may be restored after evidence of
   correction, completion of review, and where relevant re-testing or scholarly
   sign-off.

Serious user harm, security incidents, or misrepresentation may move directly to
restriction or suspension. To respond to a notice or request reinstatement,
contact <developers@quran.com>.

**Reconsideration.** A partner may request clarification or reconsideration by
submitting updated evidence to <developers@quran.com>. Decisions involving user
safety, content integrity, security, or legal obligations remain at Quran
Foundation's discretion.

## FAQ

**Can my app charge for a service?**
Yes, in context. The product should provide genuine user value, describe charges
honestly, respect content obligations, and avoid using Quranic material as a
superficial marketing vehicle. Commercial content use may require separate
written permission.

**Can we show ads or accept donations?**
Yes, if transparent, proportionate, and respectful of the user experience.
Intrusive, deceptive, or contextually inappropriate promotion can affect
eligibility or visibility.

**Does every app need OAuth?**
No. Content-only apps are the normal starting point at Verified Listing App and
can reach Vision Aligned App status without OAuth2. OAuth becomes central for
Transformational status,
and Quran Foundation OAuth as the sole backend authentication is required for
Featured placement. Where OAuth is used, request only the permissions you truly
need and explain the benefit before consent.

**Is a directory card an endorsement?**
No. A listing helps users discover an independent product. It does not mean
Quran Foundation owns the app, provides its support, or endorses every current
and future feature. A badge or label applies only to the checks described for
it.

**What is the difference between an app partner and a content partner?**
App partners build user-facing experiences and are reviewed for product, data,
support, and ecosystem fit. Content partners carry additional source, rights,
attribution, licensing, and usage considerations, and the review tracks are
distinct where those obligations differ.

**Do I still use the request-access form?**
No. API credentials, environments, redirect URIs, and scope requests are now
handled in the [Developer Console](https://dev-console.quran.foundation/projects). Existing
clients created before the console can be claimed into it.

## Glossary

- **Indexed App**: searchable-only working status; all governance requirements
  met, visibility limited until integration depth and impact grow.
- **Verified Listing App**: the baseline visible listing; content-API apps with
  full attribution and confirmed provenance.
- **Vision Aligned App**: Verified Listing App status plus mission and vision
  alignment and a deliberate path toward meaningful continuity.
- **Transformational App**: the highest status; OAuth-implemented, deeply
  aligned apps that Quran Foundation collaborates with most closely.
- **QF SSO**: one Quran Foundation account that signs users into participating
  apps with a continued journey through the user-related APIs.
- **Featured placement**: temporary editorial promotion; selected, not applied
  for; requires Quran Foundation OAuth as the sole backend user authentication.
- **Scope**: a specific permission an app requests to access user data.
- **Prelive**: the testing environment whose credentials the Developer Console
  issues alongside production.
- **Content source**: where an app's Quranic or scholarly material comes from;
  declared in the listing package.
- **Reviewed label**: a badge (Verified Listing App, Vision Aligned App, or
  Transformational App) reflecting only the checks described for it.
